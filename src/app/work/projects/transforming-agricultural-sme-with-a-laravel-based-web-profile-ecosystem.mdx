## Overview

In this community service initiative (Pengabdian Pada Masyarakat), my team and I focused on driving digital transformation for Sentra Durian Tegal, a local SME specialized in premium fruits and agricultural saplings[cite: 1]. Facing severe growth constraints due to an unstrategic physical location and highly conventional offline marketing, the business lacked a modern digital footprint. 

To bridge this gap, we engineered a custom, high-fidelity web profile ecosystem from scratch. The solution completely migrated their operations into a professional online storefront, allowing them to expand market reach and manage their digital presence autonomously.

## Key Features

![Sentra Durian Tegal System Dashboard](/images/projects/project-02/image3.png)

- **Dynamic Product Catalog**: Engineered a structured showcase displaying premium durian varieties and botanical saplings with comprehensive attributes including descriptions, pricing tiering, and real-time availability tags.
- **Centralized Admin Control Panel**: Developed a robust backend administrative dashboard that empowers the business owner to seamlessly manage gallery assets, modify menu inventories, and moderate incoming user testimonials.
- **Traffic & Visitor Analytics**: Built an internal real-time analytics module within the admin dashboard to track daily, weekly, and monthly visitor metrics—capturing data such as IP logs, timestamps, active user-agents, and precise page hit patterns.
- **Interactive Social Messaging Core**: Integrated structured customer feedback forms and direct communication routing to convert digital traffic and online leads into direct interactive sales channels with the business owner.
- **High-Fidelity Responsive Design**: Designed and built an optimized, device-agnostic user interface to ensure seamless navigation, fluid performance, and asset rendering across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.

## Technologies Used

- **Laravel Framework**: Applied PHP MVC architecture for clean routing, secure server-side logic, and reliable Blade templating compilation.
- **MySQL Database**: Structured relational data schemas to optimize product metadata, secure administrator authentication, and handle analytical log storage efficiently.
- **Google Search Console (SEO)**: Leveraged modern production indexing pipelines to ensure the platform is discoverable and ranks effectively on Google Search Engine Results Pages (SERPs) for target local keywords.
- **Production Hosting & Deployment**: Configured DNS, server environments, and domain provisioning to launch the live platform under an official commercial web extension.

## Challenges and Learnings

The definitive technical bottleneck of this project was engineering around the **digital literacy gap**. Developing an overly intricate system would defeat the purpose if the non-technical stakeholder could not maintain it independently. Therefore, our architecture focused heavily on UI simplicity, explicit user feedback loops, and bulletproof security constraint handling. 

I mastered the ability to abstract complex backend mechanics into a clean, intuitive, drag-and-drop administrative environment. Furthermore, managing production-ready live server configurations and troubleshooting index matching rules significantly refined my full-stack capabilities and network architecture understanding.

## Outcome

![Sentra Durian Tegal Product Catalog](/images/projects/project-02/image4.png)

The deployed web profile successfully engineered a solid digital identity for Sentra Durian Tegal, effectively breaking their geographical isolation and scaling visibility to a wider regional audience. The platform successfully indexed live on Google Search engine results. 

Most importantly, through strategic hands-on technical training sessions, we achieved a sustainable transition of ownership, giving the client total autonomy to update their digital storefront and scale their modern agricultural marketing practices independently.
